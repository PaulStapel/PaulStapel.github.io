> "I get knocked down, but I get up again" [Chumbawamba]

Not so long ago (maybe about an hour or so), I tragically died. I'm happy to share the news that I have been reincarnated into this world. In fact, 
I am better than ever, up and at it once more. After a long break and the experience of dying, there is a lot I want to explore and learn again. Life is far from over, and I plan to give it my all to really start living it to its fullest once more. 

I'd like to give a bit of a status update, reflect on the things that are keeping me busy right now, and look at what lies ahead in the near forseeable (am I the only one that thinks this word is spelled wrong when it isn't?) future. Right now, I am munching on some 100% cocoa dark chocolate (or "chocolate: hardcore edition") and just thinking about how grateful I am for the opportunities I have in this life. It has inspired me to write down these thoughts. 

First of all, for the major update in my life, I actually have a girlfriend now. We met in Japan, and her name is 陈蝶, and she is from 宁波, China. Without going into too much detail, I really am grateful for how understanding to me she is regarding my complex inner mental world, while also being able to challenge me and push me out of my comfort zone. We are still learning to navigate the inticracies of a long-distance relationship, but I am currently very very hopeful about this relationship. 

Besides this, I have begun working on my masters degree in Theoretical Physics and Mathematics. This will be a 2.5 year track, where I hope to learn a lot about the mathematics and physics necessary for creating awesome technology and models regarding computers. Currently, the master is really not all too difficult yet, but I do enjoy the slight increase in challenge (time for another piece of "chocolate: the reckoning"). A major challenge will not really be the courses I enroll in, but how I will use the time during this education to look for the next step. This next step will be quite a big one that will decide a large part of my career. Because of this, I hope to explore all possibilities and avenues during this phase of my life. 

To make this concretely, I wish to expend my responsibilities at work, pursue academics more directly during my education and also start working and building my own stuff more adamantly. This holy trifecta of paths will show me much more clearly which way my life will go in the future. Though I don't want to share too much how each of these paths will go exactly, as I don't think I should make any promises in this regard, I do look forward to exploring all of this a lot more. 

Then, in terms of paulstapel.com, I wish to keep writing my meditations, particles and recipes like I have done before. I will try to make drops a regular occurence again as well, as I really like the idea of writing about daily life and the small things that happen to me, though I am unsure if I can keep up the work on these. As a final note, I'd like to start dabbling in more creative writing, in the form of short stories. These may be about whatever I set my mind on, but I think they could really be a nice creative exercise to keep me busy. I might be sharing these on social media as well, though don't expect them all too soon ;). 

In terms of my body, I have actually pivoted focus of exercise a little. I have noticed that, due to injury, I have become a bit scared to lift really heavy weights. Because of this, I will try to train more for muscle growth, appearance, flexibility, stamina and, as a result, a durable body. This will be a project for the coming year, and I am curious to see if I will be able to make my body "feel more secure". In addition to going to the gym, I plan to play volleyball and do yoga. 

Having a long distance girlfriend will mean travelling a lot as well (oh no! what a cruel fate!). In doing so, I hope to not only travel for fun and for her the sake of being together, but also to expand my horizon both carreer wise as well as culturally. Having a Chinese girlfriend also means plenty of opportunities to improve my Mandarin, and I hope to unlock a new side of her inner world through being able to keep up for her main language. 

Finally, I would love to get a better balance for my free time, finding time to do chores, visit friends, game and relax. I feel like, sometimes, I have been taking more short breaks in between things, especially now that I message my girlfriend a bunch as well. Then, I'd like to regain my focus a bit, not by ignoring her, but by making sure to have room to actually engage in conversation instead of having to multi-task or be distracted by different things. This goes for other past-times as well, where I just hope to get in a good flow of studying, working, creating and relaxing once more. The future is bright, and I have much to look forward to. So let's keep working hard, ok? 加油！
