> "May we be blessed by a death that comes to all but our lives" [Me] 

It is with great saddness that I, Paul Engbert Stapel, share with you the news of my death. It was a life where years felt like days, and days like eternities. A life of joys and of greater joys. Sadness, too, is but an expression of joy, after all. And now, it is with my greatest sadness that I have this chance to sing my own requiem -- oh how rare it is to be able to sing your own requiem. Truly, even in death has nothing but luck been brought upon me. Then, let me make haste, as I have not a moment to spare and not a moment to waste.  

---

Oh lord above, grant him mercy. \
Your subject who lies here to die. \
This death was not nature's calling. \
It was you, and it was I. 

His passion ever-overflowing. \
Highest, higher, ever high. \
This death was not nature's calling. \
It was you, and it was I. 

Rebellious ever was his nature. \
His own rules doth ne'er apply. \
This death was not nature's calling. \
It was you, and it was I. 

He's been ever the deciever. \
To degrees he shant deny. \
This death was not nature's calling. \
It was you, and it was I. 

Hear him too, oh brothers, sisters. \
For this is his last goodbye. \
This death was not nature's calling. \
It was you, and it was I. 

Does one not see oh so clearly. \
With their old and weary eye. \
This death was not nature's calling. \
It was you, and it was I.

Was there not one friend or foe. \
There to teach or edify? \
This death was not nature's calling. \
It was you, and it was I.

Damned you are in all your essence. \
Ne'er to hurt and ne'er to pry. \
This death was not nature's calling. \
It was you, and it was I.

Yet still do not mourn his passing. \
Do not shed a tear or cry. \
This death was not nature's calling. \
It was you, and it was I. 

Let his soul just rest in piece. \
Let his spirit simply fly. \
This death was not nature's calling. \
It was you, and it was I.

Perhaps the daybreak of tomorrow. \
Brings an end to death her sorrow. \
And engulfed in new flames doth a new life also lie. \
A brighter morning in the soul we'll christen - You and I.
