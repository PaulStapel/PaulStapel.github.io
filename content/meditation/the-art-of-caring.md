> "Love? Maybe. But true love? Only for the brave. Most people confuse comfort with passion and call it love. Weaklings" [Nietzsche]

Love has been on my mind lately. Not just my love for the world, for science, for my girlfriend or for the friends that surround me. No, love as in the burning flames of passion. Have I not spoken about loving all these things? Have I not shouted my love from the rooftops? Then where is that passion in my actions? How can I say that the flames of desire consume me the light shining from me merely flickers? It is the duty of all that burns to turn ash. I have turned ash and man and ash again, but by what flame. I am afraid the rivers of life doth make this firewood wet, but even wet wood can burn under bright enough flames!

For the world, let my flame consume in every waking moment. Let me worship at the alter, see the world as it is, and what it could be. Yes, only those devout enough to truly worship this world can see that becoming ashes on its soil is the greatest honor one can wish for. Then, let me become ash and man and ash again, on its soil! Let me always keep in mind the passion I have for everyone and everything on this earth, and may I not only say I care, but also show I care. May I be brave enough to let this love consume me. 

And in my science, may I not be tempted with waves of "good enough". Like a frog over a slow boiling pot, eventually it will cook me. But I wish not to be cooked, I wish to be burned! These words of good enough, when have they ever amounted to anything but the will of the weak? Let me be clear, there is no weakness in being weak of will! Oh, how little we realize that weakness is the least of our weaknesses. No, our fault lies in desiring strongly but loving weakly! How many scientists I have seen that desire to love without desiring to be consumed by this love. You can have likes and you can have loves, but don't you dare pretend to love when you merely like! It is an insult to the loved and an insult to love itself. 

On the bonfire, can we even distinguish fire from firewood? How, then, is it that so many of these so called lovers are so easily distinguished from the love they preach? Object and subject not one and the same? I'd spit on you. In fact, my spit alone would be enough to extinguish such pitiful flames, you so-called lovers. There is no shame in being wet, but much shame in being drenched with spit of men that look down on you. Can a bonfire be extinguished with spit? I'd love to see you try!

So then, why is it that I feel this burning feeling, yet don't seem on fire. Am I containing the fire inside me, afraid it will burn others? I don't think this is the case. I am afraid of burning myself. But to turn ash and man and ash again, one must get burned on the fires of passions. There is no other way! Let me live life fully then, experience everything it has to offer, sting and burn and cry out in pain over everything I get to experience. In the end, I will have lived a life worth living, a life worth dying. 

But these flames, they don't grow ignite spontaneously. My firewood is there, so let me combust it! Let me care about everything I do all the more deeply. Let my fire be seen from miles away, and let the smoke reach even further! And my ash, may it bring forward new life, over and over and over again. 

The ultimate way of not caring about what happens is to stop caring about not caring as well. Burn it all away until merely ash remains...
