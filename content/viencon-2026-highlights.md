This year, I attended viencon. Not only was this my first convention, but it was also the first time I have ever tried cosplay (dart monkey counts, right?????). As this con had a lot of firsts for me, I wanted to share some of the highlights from this convention and all of the firsts I was lucky enough to experience. 

## Here, I experienced my first: 
- Convention (I must say that most activities AROUND the con where better than the activities WITHIN the con).
- Trip to the ER (dislocated my arm again <3).
- Maid cafe (The omurice was alright, but the vibe was cool). 
- Cosplay (I was a dart monkey from BTD).
- Malibu fristi (it was alright, I guess). 
- After party (They where all really short though, and I was pretty tired ngl). 
- Swimming pool rave (This was actually really sick). 
- Live rap battle (The bars where straight whatever the opposite of fire is). 
- Beer tasting (One of the highlights, shoutout to Luigi and the host!).
- Horror experience (Being chased by father smurf).
